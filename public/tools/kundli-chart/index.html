Vimshottari Dasha

These charts use whole-sign houses, sidereal zodiac (Lahiri ayanamsa) and mean lunar nodes - calculated, not copied. The Navamsa (D9) is the classical ninth-harmonic chart, traditionally weighed for marriage, dharma and a planet's underlying strength. The Vimshottari dasha (120-year cycle) is reckoned from the Moon's nakshatra and shown to three layers - Mahadasha, Antardasha and Pratyantardasha. A true reading weighs dasha, divisional charts and transits together: explore the Nakshatra guides, Mahadasha guides, or book a consultation.
