Your Aura Fragrance

Two layers of scent live in every chart. The D1 sets your projected aura — what people sense as you enter. The D9 holds your soul scent — the base that settles on skin and the layer to lean on when you want to lift your presence. Your Nakshatra is the heart note that makes it unmistakably you.

This uses whole-sign houses, the sidereal zodiac (Lahiri ayanamsa) and mean lunar nodes — the same astronomy as a standard kundli, computed from the real sky at your birth. The Navamsa (D9) is the classical ninth-harmonic chart. The fragrance mapping itself is a symbolic, creative framework — meant to be meaningful and fun, not a scientific or medical claim.
