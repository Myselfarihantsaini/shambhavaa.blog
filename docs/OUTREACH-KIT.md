# Outreach kit — copy, fill the [brackets], send

Backlinks have to be earned from other websites. Everything below is ready to copy. Do **3 outreach actions a day**; most replies come after 2–4 weeks.

---

## 1. Guest-post pitch email (English)

Find sites with Google searches like:
`"vedic astrology" "write for us"` · `"astrology" "guest post guidelines"` · `"spirituality blog" "submit a guest post"` · `"jyotish" "contribute"`

**Subject:** Guest article idea for [Site name]: [Topic]

> Hi [Name],
>
> I read your piece on [their article title] — especially the part about [one specific detail]. I write about Vedic astrology at shambhavaa.blog, where I publish house-by-house planet guides and dasha explainers.
>
> I'd like to write an original article for [Site name]. Three ideas that would fit your readers:
>
> 1. How to read a Mahadasha in three steps (D1, D9 and transits)
> 2. Saturn in the 7th house: why delay is not denial in marriage
> 3. The 3-6-10-11 house rule for career growth
>
> Each would be 1,500+ words, written only for you and not published elsewhere. In return I'd ask for a short author bio with one link to my site.
>
> Recent work: https://shambhavaa.blog/mars/mars-in-all-12-houses-vedic-astrology/
>
> Thanks for reading,
> Arihant Saini
> https://shambhavaa.blog

**Follow up once** after 7 days, then move on.

## Never do

- Buy backlinks or "SEO packages" (Fiverr, link farms, PBNs) — Google penalises the whole site.
- Paste the same link-only comment on many blogs.
- Swap links with unrelated sites ("I link you, you link me") at scale.
